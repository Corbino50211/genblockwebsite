Minecraft: Java Edition 26.3

How to play

No waiting for an admin to come online. Everything runs on its own.

Join

You get a free 100x100 plot the first time you join. Type /plot to go there.

Visit the shack

At spawn, the Oak, Stone and Dirt gens are free for everyone. Mine logs and cobblestone, then craft your first tools.

Buy a gen

Bring 192 oak logs to the Gen Shop villager for your first Coal Gen.

Mine & upgrade

Place gens on your plot. Mine the block on top and it grows right back. Save up for the next tier.

Generators

Every gen grows one block on top of it. Mine it and it comes back. Buy them from the Gen Shop villager with the ores you've mined.

Sneak + right-click a gen with an empty hand to pick it up and move it. You need the right pickaxe to get drops, same as in vanilla.

Plots & friends

Your plot is yours. Show it off, or build it together.

Your plot

100x100 blocks of flat grass with roads around it. Only you can pick up the gens on your plot.

Visitors

Anyone can come look with /plot visit <name>, but visitors can't break, place or open anything.

Friends

Friends can build and mine gens on each other's plots. Send a request with /friend add <name>.

Block Shop

The Block Shop villager trades building blocks for ores, so you never have to wait for an admin:

  • All 16 concrete colors, glass, wool and grass
  • Spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak and cherry logs
  • Bricks, stone bricks, deepslate, tuff, quartz and prismarine
  • Glowstone and sea lanterns for light, plus bread and steak

Commands

That's all of them.

/plotTeleport to your plot
/plot visit <player>Visit someone's plot
/friend add <player>Send a friend request (they click [Accept])
/friend remove <player>Remove a friend
/friend listSee your friends
/spawnBack to spawn, the shack and the shops
